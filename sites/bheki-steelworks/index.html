How we work

Measured twice. Welded once. Finished like it matters.

1

Measure & quote

Bheki comes to you, measures the opening, talks through styles and sends a written quote. Usually same day.

2

Fabricate

Cut, squared, jigged and welded in the workshop. Every seam ground flush before it goes anywhere near paint.

3

Finish

De-greased, primed and painted or powder-coated. The finish is what you look at every day, so it gets the time it needs.

4

Install & hand over

Fitted level, tested, site cleaned. You get the keys and Bheki's number. If anything needs adjusting, he comes back.

About Bheki

The man who answers the phone is the man who does the work

"If I wouldn't put it on my own house, it doesn't leave my workshop."

Bheki Ndlovu is a steel fabricator and handyman who has built his reputation one gate at a time. He is particular about the things most people only notice when they're wrong: a gate that drags, a bar that sits crooked, a weld that was painted over instead of ground.

He works hard, keeps his word on dates, and makes himself available when customers need him. No call centre, no sub-contractors you've never met. You deal with Bheki from the first measurement to the last coat of paint.
